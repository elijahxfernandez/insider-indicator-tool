HOW TO READ IT

Five states. One lens.

Positive divergence
Price falls; observed ownership activity rises.
Negative divergence
Price rises; observed ownership activity falls.
Confirmation
Price and ownership activity align in direction.
Quiet accumulation
Ownership activity rises while price stays within the flat band.
Mixed/neutral
The measured directions do not form an aligned pattern.

Price flat band: less than ±2%. Partial analysis uses Form 4 and price. Form 3 and P-purchase detail add context, not extra votes.
